DRYHOLE is an on-chain NFT ecosystem built around Genesis, Epochs, Drilling and an internal economy.

Core system

Genesis

Genesis is the second-generation DRYHOLE NFT system. DRYHOLE V1 holders receive Genesis access based on their V1 holdings. Genesis uses generations from Gen 1 to Gen 5.

A selected Genesis allocation cannot simultaneously be used for Drill.

Core system

Epoch

DRYHOLE operates through recurring daily Epochs. 1 Epoch = 1 day. Epoch reset: 23:00 UTC. Genesis participants accumulate Points during an active Epoch. At the end of the Epoch:

01

CALCULATE

Total Points are calculated.

02

SHARE

Each participant receives a share of the reward pool proportional to their Points.

03

RESET

The Epoch resets.

04

RESTART

A new Epoch begins.

01What is DRYHOLE

DRYHOLE is an on-chain NFT ecosystem combining:

  • V1 NFTs
  • V2 / Genesis NFTs
  • Genesis
  • Epochs
  • Drill
  • DRYHOLE token
  • Swap
  • Market
  • Stake
  • Burn

The protocol is designed around recurring Epochs and NFT utility.

02NFT Generations

DRYHOLE V1 is the original DRYHOLE NFT collection.

DRYHOLE V2 / Genesis is the second-generation NFT system.

V1 and V2 have different utilities.

V1

  • Drill
  • Burn

V2 / Genesis

  • Genesis
  • Drill

03Genesis

Genesis is organized into generations. Each generation requires a fixed number of NFTs.

GenerationNFT Requirement
Gen 15 NFTs
Gen 210 NFTs
Gen 320 NFTs
Gen 430 NFTs
Gen 550 NFTs

The requirement must be fully satisfied to select a generation.

Example: a user holding 40 NFTs who selects Gen 4 allocates 30 NFTs to Genesis, leaving 10 NFTs available for other utility such as Drill.

NFTs allocated to Genesis cannot simultaneously be used for Drill.

Higher generations provide higher Point multipliers. Finalized rates so far:

  • Gen 1 — 10 Points / hour
  • Gen 2 — 30 Points / hour
  • Gen 3 to Gen 5 — Not finalized.

04Epoch System

1 day = 1 Epoch. The Epoch resets at 23:00 UTC. Points accumulate during an active Epoch.

At the end of an Epoch:

  1. Total Points are calculated.
  2. Each participant's share is determined from their Points.
  3. The reward pool is distributed proportionally.
  4. Points reset.
  5. A new Epoch begins.

Example: User A has 1,000 Points and User B has 3,000 Points, for a total of 4,000 Points. User A receives 25% of the reward pool and User B receives 75%.

05Points & Rewards

Genesis Points determine a participant's share of the Epoch reward pool.

Reward amounts are not fixed for every Epoch. The interface currently shows Epoch 1 with a $270 display value, approximately 0.1 ETH at a reference price. This is a display value, not a permanent protocol rule.

Future Epoch reward amounts: Not finalized.

06Drill

Drill is a core NFT utility. Both V1 and V2 / Genesis NFTs can participate in Drill.

NFTs allocated to Genesis cannot simultaneously participate in Drill.

Drill rates and rewards: Not finalized.

07DRYHOLE Token

DRYHOLE is the native ecosystem token. It is used throughout the internal economy, including Swap, Market and Stake.

Token supply, emission and distribution: Not finalized.

08Swap

Swap is the token exchange layer. It contains three functions:

  • Buy — swap a supported base asset into DRYHOLE.
  • Sell — swap DRYHOLE into a supported base asset.
  • Convert — convert Genesis Points into DRYHOLE.

Buy and Sell are token exchange functions. Convert is not an NFT marketplace and not a token swap pair.

Supported assets, rates and contract addresses: Not finalized.

09Market

The Market allows users to buy and sell DRYHOLE V1 and V2 NFTs.

The native DRYHOLE token is the Market currency.

10Stake

Stake is the DRYHOLE token staking system.

  • Reward rates: Not finalized.
  • Staking duration: Not finalized.
  • Reward assets: Not finalized.

11Burn

Burn is a V1-only utility. V1 NFTs can be burned through the Burn system.

Burn rewards and rates: Not finalized.
