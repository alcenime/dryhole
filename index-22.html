Introduction

DRYHOLE is built around a repeating Epoch cycle. Every Epoch is one complete protocol cycle, and 1 day = 1 Epoch.

The same cycle is used across the whole system. Drilling, token activity and staking all operate inside the same protocol rhythm, so each part of DRYHOLE can be understood by reference to the Epoch it belongs to.

EPOCH

1 Epoch = 1 day. The Epoch is the basic, synchronized time unit of DRYHOLE. Every protocol system operates around the same Epoch cycle, so activity in one part of the system can always be placed in the same timeline as the rest.

When an Epoch ends, the current cycle settles and the next Epoch begins. Epochs give drilling a defined window, let activity and rewards be measured by cycle, and keep the ecosystem in step. No further Epoch rules are defined here.

01Overview

DRYHOLE is an on-chain drilling protocol. Holders use DRYHOLE NFTs in a drilling system, drilling connects to a protocol token, and the token can take part in a staking system that uses ETH as the reward asset. Every part of the system follows a shared Epoch cycle.

The app will be organized into six areas: Home, Collection, Drill, Stake, Swap and Market. This document describes the intended role of each one. It is a working draft, and anything marked as in development is a concept only.

02Epoch system

An Epoch is one complete protocol cycle and lasts one day. 1 Epoch = 1 day. It is the synchronized time unit of DRYHOLE: drilling, token activity and staking are all placed in the Epoch they belong to.

At the end of an Epoch the current cycle is settled and the next one begins. The exact start time, time zone convention and settlement mechanism are still in development. No additional Epoch rules should be assumed.

03NFT

The DRYHOLE NFT is the drilling asset and the entry point into the protocol. Holding an eligible NFT is what allows participation in Drill. The NFT represents the ability to drill. It does not represent a promised result or reward.

The full set of DRYHOLE NFTs is viewed in Collection. Eligibility criteria, total supply and mint details are still in development.

04Drill

Drill is the system in which an NFT takes part in drilling during an active Epoch. Conceptually the drill starts at the surface, moves into the subsurface, and produces a reading that is unknown until the process completes.

A drill ends in one of two outcomes: a discovery or a dry result. Both are valid protocol outcomes. How results are determined, how they relate to token generation, and any rates or depths are still in development.

05Token

The DRYHOLE token is the economic utility layer. Drilling connects the NFT layer to the token layer, so that participation has a use beyond holding an NFT. The token is the asset used by Stake and Swap.

How the token is generated is defined by the final protocol rules. Total supply, emission, amount per drill, price and the contract address are still in development.

06Stake

Stake is the token utility layer. The intended design is TOKEN → STAKE → ETH: the token takes part in the staking system and ETH is the reward asset.

Staking follows the Epoch cycle, and rewards and settlement are associated with Epochs. APR, ETH amounts, lock duration and reward formulas are still in development. No return is guaranteed.

07Swap

Swap is the exchange interface for supported assets and tokens inside the protocol, including the DRYHOLE token. It is currently a concept only and is not connected to any on-chain functionality.

Supported assets, pairs, fees and rates are still in development.

08Market

Market is the marketplace layer for DRYHOLE NFTs, where they can be bought and sold. It is separate from Collection. Collection is for viewing and discovering NFTs, and Market is for buying and selling them.

Listing rules, pricing and fees are still in development.

09Economy

The DRYHOLE economy is the way the layers connect: NFT → DRILL → TOKEN → STAKE → ETH, repeated every Epoch. The NFT is the entry point, the token is the utility layer, and ETH is the intended reward asset for staking.

Tokenomics, reward sources, emission and any economic parameters are still in development and are not described here. Participation involves risk. Nothing in this document is financial advice or a promise of return.
